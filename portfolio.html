About

I love the logic nobody sees behind a system, and I stress-test what I build until it bends. At Zetrix AI and MYEG I replaced an Excel process with a website now in production, used by thousands of people.

I have loved learning since the beginning. In matriculation I was trying to avoid biology, so I picked computer science as my elective, and that is where it hooked me. I started with pseudocode on paper and wrote code by hand in a book. Now I can write Java from memory. Wanting to learn more is what took me to UKM.

Since then, the part of coding I love most is the algorithm: the hard logic under a system. I have competed at PPKOMP three years running, with bronze in 2023, silver in 2024 and gold in 2025. Once something works, I brute-force it with bad input and heavy load to see where it bends.

My months in IT Risk Assurance at PwC taught me the ITGC framework, and I apply it in code: password configuration, batch job scheduling and disaster recovery. HealthSecure, my final year project, earned the Digital Innovation Award (KID). Away from the keyboard I play chess and keep learning.
